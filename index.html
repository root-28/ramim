About Me

I am a cybersecurity researcher and offensive security practitioner with a strong interest in penetration testing, vulnerability research, intrusion detection, IoT security, machine learning, deep learning, and AI-driven cybersecurity. I graduated with a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (Specialization in Cyber Security) from Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT-AP), India.

My work combines practical offensive-security techniques with research-oriented approaches to develop and evaluate security solutions for modern networks, IoT environments, and intelligent systems. I am particularly interested in research opportunities involving cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, network security, and security of emerging technologies.
